Clips Uploader

A private upload tool for one YouTube channel.

Clips Uploader is a small personal tool. It uploads finished videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, to its owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

It is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts to anyone else and does not access any other person's YouTube channel or data.
